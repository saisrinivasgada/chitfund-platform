The Basics

What is a Chit Fund?

👥

A Group Savings Plan

A group of friends, family, or colleagues come together. Each person pays a fixed amount every month. Everyone benefits.

🏆

One Winner Every Month

Every month, one person in the group gets the whole pot of money. By the end, every single person has won once.

🔒

Safe & Organised

ChitWise keeps everything organised — who paid, who won, and who gets paid next. No confusion, no missing money.

📱

Always on Your Phone

Members can check their balance, see payment history, and request cash pickup — all from a phone app, anytime.

Example
10 Members × ₹5,000/month
= ₹50,000 each month · One winner gets the full ₹50,000
Duration
10 Months
Every person wins exactly once over 10 months

People & Roles

Who's Involved?

👑

Admin

The owner. Sets up chit groups, manages staff, oversees all money, and has full control of the platform.

🗂️

Manager

Runs day-to-day operations. Opens monthly draws, manages payouts, and coordinates the field team.

🏃

Staff / Worker

Goes to members' homes to collect cash payments. Reports back to the manager after every pickup.

🧑‍💼

Member

The chit participant. Pays monthly installments and eventually wins the pot. Has their own mobile app.

💡

Simple for members. Powerful for organisers. ChitWise keeps each person's view clean and relevant — members only see what they need, admin sees everything.

Step by Step

How ChitWise Works

1

Create the Chit Group

Admin sets up a new chit — how many members, how much per month, and how long it runs. Members are added to the group.

2

Members Pay Every Month

Each month, members pay their installment — by cash (collected at home by a staff worker), bank transfer, or UPI. ChitWise records every payment instantly.

3

The Monthly Draw

At the end of each month, the manager opens a draw. One member is selected as the winner (either by lot or by a pre-agreed schedule). The result is recorded in ChitWise.

4

Winner Gets Paid

The admin or manager processes the payout. The winner receives the full amount — tracked in ChitWise so everyone can verify it. Can be partial disbursements too.

5

Repeat Until Everyone Wins

The process repeats every month until every member has won exactly once. The chit is then marked complete. Full history is kept forever.

ChitWise 10
Admin Action

How to Create a Chit Group

1

Go to Chits → New Chit

Admin clicks "New Chit" from the Chits page. A form opens to fill in the chit details.

2

Set Name & Amount

Give the chit a name (e.g. "Family Gold 2024") and set the monthly contribution amount per member.

3

Choose Type & Duration

Pick Open Draw (random winner each month) or Reservation (pre-assigned turns). Set total number of draws.

4

Add Members & Org Slots

Add participating members. Optionally reserve slots for the organisation (admin-held positions).

5

Activate the Chit

Once all members are confirmed, change the status to ACTIVE. The chit is now live and collecting payments.

6

Members Get Notified

All enrolled members receive an instant notification on their phone that the chit has started.
